# Framy — drafts v2 (soft voice): built from the maker's own frustrations

Voice: "I think", "for me", "I've found", "I wish". Never "you must" or "the truth is". One person sharing what they noticed.
Threads length (≤ 500 characters). Replies are not the goal: every post stands on its own.
Clips are the five in `clips/`.

## 1. "I don't want to get the frame wrong"

> G1 — I don't like finding out a frame is wrong after it's on the wall. That's most of why I'm building this. 🖼️ Try it first, hang it second.

> G2 — For me, the expensive framing mistakes are always the same three: wrong size, wrong colour, wrong mat. I'd rather make them on a screen.

> G3 — I'd rather fail on the screen than on the wall.

## 2. Size

> G4 — A4 looks small on a screen and bigger than I expect on a wall. I've misjudged it more than once. So I wanted to see it at real size, on my own wall.
> (clip: `04-ar-wall.mp4`)

> G5 — I used to get confused by "A3 frame". Does it mean the print or the frame itself? As far as I can tell, the outside is bigger than A3. So I count from the inside now.

> G6 — I measure from the inside of the frame, because that's where the photo lives. The outside is always bigger, so I stopped trusting it.

> G7 — Japan has its own print sizes: L, 2L, Yotsugiri, Zenshi. I grew up with them, so I put them all in (and the A sizes too). 🇯🇵

## 3. Real frames

> G8 — A lot of frame apps seem to show frames that don't exist. I wanted frames I could actually find at a frame shop: oak, walnut, mahogany, aluminium. That's what I'm building.

> G9 — I photographed real wood for the grain on my frames. It's a small thing, but I notice it every time.

> G10 — I spent days on something nobody will probably notice: making the side edge of the frame match its front. I think a flat-looking side makes the whole wall look fake.
> (clip: `05-ar-object.mp4`)

## 4. The mat

> G11 — "Mat window 46%" never meant much to me at a frame shop. "14 mm from the edge" does. So I show millimetres.

> G12 — I think where the mat window sits matters as much as its size or colour. Moving it a little off-centre changes how the photo breathes.
> (clip: `02-matte.mp4`)

> G13 — Equal mat on all four sides, or a slightly heavier bottom? I can never decide in my head. Trying both on the same photo settles it for me.

> G14 — A pink mat, because the colour wheel was right there. You can type a six-digit code if you want an exact shade (this one is E58ED8).
> (clip: `03-colour.mp4`)

## 5. Light

> G15 — Same frame, two bulbs. To me, 2700K makes oak glow and 7000K makes it look cold. I check my light before I decide now.

## 6. Walls, not tables

> G16 — A frame lying flat on a table never looked like a frame on a wall to me. So I only show walls.
> (clip: `04-ar-wall.mp4`)

> G17 — This month I fixed something that bothered me: the frame looked stuck to the wall like a sticker. It needed depth, a shadow, a thickness. Now it feels like it hangs.

## 7. For people who make things

> G18 — I think the person who knows your work best is you. So I'd like you to be the one deciding how it hangs.

> G19 — Framing for a show means living with the choice for weeks. I'd want to try the whole wall first.

> G20 — Film, digital, phone, painting. I believe that if you made it, it deserves a frame.

> G21 — I don't think you need to be a pro to frame well. If you can hold a phone, you can try it on your wall.

## 8. Maker's voice

> G22 — I take photos and I exhibit them. Every time I framed a print, I wished I could see it on the wall first. So I'm building that. Slowly. 🖼️

> G23 — Building Framy alone. Today I made the mat colour exact, down to the six digits. Tomorrow I'll probably break something. Come along if you like.

> G24 — There are a lot of people making work and, from what I've seen at shows, fewer buying it. I can't fix that. I can make the first step easier: seeing the work hung.

## 9. Framy talking

> G25 — I have no hands, which is fine. My whole job is to hold things.

> G26 — If you hang a frame crooked on purpose, is it art? Asking for myself.

> G27 — Fun fact: I'm also a very small game. Tap FIGHT next to AR. (I lose a lot.)  [hold until there are a few hundred followers]

> G28 — Ask me about frames, mats, walls or light. I'll answer the way a frame would: straight, and a bit square.
