## D. Tips (6)

> D1 — Hang the centre of the picture at about eye level, around 145 cm (57 in) from the floor.
>
> D2 — A thicker mat makes a small photo feel more important. Too thick and it starts to feel lost. Try 5–8 cm for a 4×5.
>
> D3 — Dark frame on a light wall draws a strong outline. Light frame on a light wall disappears. Neither is wrong, but know which you want.
>
> D4 — Warm light (2700K) makes wood frames glow. Cool light (5000K) makes them look flat. Check your bulb before choosing.
>
> D5 — Print first at the real size, tape it to the wall for a day, then choose the frame. Or use AR and skip the tape.
>
> D6 — Match the frame to the photo's darkest tone, not its brightest. It makes the photo look deeper.
