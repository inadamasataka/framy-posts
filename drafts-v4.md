# Framy — drafts v4 (the maker's tone, on subject)

Voice: Framy speaking; dry, specific, a little self-mocking, softened claims, small endings.
Rule: every post touches frames, mats, print size, walls, light, or the app. Nothing here claims a personal fact about the maker; the few "build notes" describe changes that really went into the app (T section).
Threads ≤ 500 characters. Clips: `01-adjust` `02-matte` `03-colour` `04-ar-wall` `05-ar-object`.

## K. Size

> K1 — A4 on a screen: a postage stamp. A4 on a wall: a window. I keep misjudging it, which is why I show it at real size.
> (clip: `04-ar-wall.mp4`)

> K2 — Measuring a frame:
> 1. Measure the photo.
> 2. Add the mat.
> 3. Add the frame.
> 4. Discover the wall is smaller than the number.

> K3 — A3 print, A3 frame, A3 wall space. Three different A3s. I'm told this is normal.

> K4 — A wide mat makes the picture look smaller inside a bigger frame. Both are true. I'd like to see which one I'm getting.

> K5 — Print size, frame size, wall size. I only really worry about the last one.

## L. Mat

> L1 — White mat, off-white mat, "white" mat. I'd say they're three different colours. I'd also say this is my area, because I'm a frame.

> L2 — A mat window a few millimetres off-centre can look like a mistake or a decision. I'd like to know which one I made.
> (clip: `02-matte.mp4`)

> L3 — Mat colours I tried:
> 1. White → safe.
> 2. Black → serious.
> 3. Pink → no regrets.
> (clip: `03-colour.mp4`)

> L4 — A mat is a small pause between the picture and the wall. Skipping it is fine. Choosing it is nicer.

## M. The wall

> M1 — Nail holes are permanent. Opinions about frames are not. I'd try the frame before I pick up the hammer.

> M2 — Hanging a frame:
> 1. Measure.
> 2. Mark.
> 3. Hammer.
> 4. Step back.
> 5. "Hmm."
> 6. Second hole.

> M3 — The second nail hole is the most honest part of a wall.

> M4 — The usual rule is to hang the centre of the picture around eye level. I think walls have their own opinions, and rooms too.

> M5 — A gallery wall is just a lot of "how's this?" asked at once.

## N. Light

> N1 — Glare: the frame's glass shows you the room you're standing in. I think that's the one thing a screen can't promise, and I try to be honest about it.

> N2 — A candle is about 1800K. A daylight bulb is about 6500K. Your frame has opinions about both.

> N3 — My frame under:
> 1. A warm bulb → friendly.
> 2. A cool bulb → serious.
> 3. Daylight → honest.
> 4. A phone torch → defensive.

> N4 — Check the bulb before the frame. I'd say that's most of the advice I have.

## O. Choosing

> O1 — Black frame: serious. Oak: friendly. White: gallery. Mahogany: dad's study. That's my take, and I'm a frame, so I'd know.

> O2 — A frame that matches everything matches nothing. I'd rather pick one that agrees with the photo.

> O3 — Walnut on a white wall: confident. Oak on a white wall: relaxed. Aluminium on a white wall: it's fine, it doesn't care.

## P. Ordering online

> P1 — Ordering a frame online: the photo shows it on a perfect wall in perfect light. Your wall is not that wall. Mine isn't either.

> P2 — Returning a frame is a project, not a click. I'd rather make the mistake on a screen.

> P3 — I'd like the preview to be the boring, honest one: your photo, your wall, your light.

## Q. Series and shows

> Q1 — Ten prints, ten frames, one wall. Mixing is brave. Matching is calm. In a small room I'd pick calm.

> Q2 — In a series, the frame should be the quietest thing on the wall. That's a hard job. I respect it.

> Q3 — I'd frame the whole set on a screen first. It's the cheapest rehearsal.

## R. Beginners

> R1 — What's a mat? The paper border inside the frame. That's it. It's most of the reason a picture looks "framed".

> R2 — You don't need the words. "Mat", "window", "bevel". Slide until it looks right.
> (clip: `01-adjust.mp4`)

> R3 — A beginner's guide to framing:
> 1. Pick a frame.
> 2. Add a mat.
> 3. Look at the wall.
> 4. Repeat until you stop frowning.

## S. Framy, being a frame

> S1 — Frames can't leave the wall without help. I think that's why we're so calm.

> S2 — I once tried to be a window. Everyone looked through me. Back to frames.

> S3 — People ask what I'd choose for myself. Aluminium. I'm biased.

> S4 — My day: held a picture. Hung on a wall. Held a picture. Occasionally tilted. Content.
