# Framy — voice and look (v1)

## Who is talking
Framy: a small friendly frame who built itself out of one maker's frustration. First person ("I"), plain English, a little playful, never salesy.
Behind Framy there is a real maker who exhibits work and framed it himself, so the posts can say what that is like.
Audience, in order: people who make things (photographers, illustrators, painters) → people who hang things (home, exhibitions) → curious beginners.

## The idea in one line
**Simulate before you hang.**
Supporting lines (use one at a time, never all together):
- Nobody knows your work better than you. Frame it your way.
- Fail on the screen, not on the wall.
- Frames you could actually buy at a frame shop.

## How it sounds
- **Soft, first-person, not declarative.** "I think", "for me", "I've found", "I wish", "I'd rather". Share what one maker noticed; do not tell people how things are or what they should do. Avoid "you must", "always", "never", "the truth is".
- Short sentences. One idea per post. Often a small confession ("I once…", "I got tired of…").
- Talk to "you". Each post should be worth reading on its own: a fact, a small tip, a confession, a clip. Questions are fine now and then, but replies are not the goal; being worth a follow is.
- Specific beats general: say "5 mm", "A3", "2700K", "oak", not "a nice frame".
- Emoji: 🖼️ is Framy's. At most two emoji per post. None in the middle of a sentence.
- No hashtag spam on Threads (one topic tag at most). Instagram: 3–5 tags at the end.

## Words to use
frame, mat, window, wall, print, real size, hang, exhibit, light, texture, edge, depth
## Words to avoid
Download now, Buy, Best, Amazing, Revolutionary, Game-changing, Must-have, Free (some frames are paid), "AI", anything about other apps being worse.

## Rules for honesty
- The app is not on the App Store yet: say "coming soon" or "in development", never "available".
- Claims must be things the app really does (frames modelled on real frame-shop frames, mat width in mm, real-size AR, light colour temperature, mat window position, wood grain photographed from real wood).
- Do not say "free". Say nothing about prices until launch.
- Never put down the pros or the beginners. The camera world bristles easily: stay curious, not superior.

## Look (for images and video text)
- Background: white or very light warm grey. The icon is on white; stay close to it.
- Colours: frame brown (#b98a55 oak, #4a2d1a walnut), ink (#1b1b1b), star yellow (#ffd23d) as the single accent.
- Font: M PLUS Rounded 1c, light (300), the same as the app's logo. Lots of space around the words.
- Framy's pixel character: only as a small accent or as the speaker of a one-liner, never as decoration on every image.
- Video text: one short English line, same position (bottom third), white text with a soft shadow, 3–4 words ("Pick a frame." "Add a mat." "Hang it on your wall.").
- Every image or clip works with the sound off.

## Post types and rhythm (per day of three)
1. One clip or card that shows the app.
2. One tip or plain fact worth saving.
3. One personal line: Framy talking, or the maker's build notes.
Never two clips in a row. At most one question a day, and often none.
